    Class VillageStructure

    A village: a well, streets running out from it, and houses and fields along them.

    The layout is worked out entirely from the village's origin and the world seed - never from the chunk being generated - because the same plan has to be produced independently by each of the dozen or so chunks the village covers. Anything that varied between them would show up as a house with two different roofs, or half a road.

    Slots are offered in a fixed order and taken if the terrain and the neighbours allow it, so the randomness decides what a village looks like without deciding whether the pieces fit.
