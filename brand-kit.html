Brand kit ​

The rules live in the brand guide. This page renders the pieces they describe from the same source the site uses, so what you see here is what ships.

Palette ​

accent--jsp-accent#5ca48aPrimary accent: buttons, highlights, active states.
forest--jsp-forest#003829Dark brand surface and light-mode link color.
deep--jsp-deep#00281dDarkest surface: tile backgrounds and shadows.
mint--jsp-mint#a9d9c6Body text on forest and the light facet of the mark.
cream--jsp-cream#efe9daLight surface and text on dark.
ink--jsp-ink#0b1713Text on light and the terminal surface.
signal--jsp-signal#ff6b3dSpot color for prompts, cursors and the core of the mark.
