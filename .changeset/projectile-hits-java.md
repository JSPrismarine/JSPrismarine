Land projectiles the way Java does. Every blow a projectile deals now pushes its target with Java's `LivingEntity.knockback`, lifting a grounded mob 0.4 rather than 0.1, and a ghast's fireball, a llama's spit and an ender pearl push too. A shulker bullet gives ten seconds of Levitation, a wither skull deals 8 (5 without a wither) and Wither II, a blaze's fireball sets its target alight, and a fireball or wither skull explodes where it lands. An ender pearl takes its thrower to where it landed and hurts a player 5, a bottle o' enchanting drops Java's 3 to 11 experience, a dragon's fireball leaves Java's cloud of Harming, and a wind charge bursts and is gone. A shulker bullet stops at the first block, and no projectile shoves the mob it passes through.
