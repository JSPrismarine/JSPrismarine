Run the evoker's fangs and fix falling blocks. Evoker fangs now exist: they bite whatever stands on them for 6 magic damage eight ticks after they rise, spare the evoker that cast them, and leave after 23 ticks. A falling block falls with Java's gravity and drag, breaks into its item when it lands in a block it cannot replace, such as a torch, and a falling anvil hurts what it lands on by 2 per block fallen past the first.
