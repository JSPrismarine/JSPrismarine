Village raids run. A player with Bad Omen who stands in a village starts one: the omen is spent, a countdown runs, and Java's waves of pillagers, vindicators, evokers, witches and ravagers spawn around the village, three, five or seven by difficulty and one more for a stronger omen, each only once the last is dead. Clearing every wave wins the raid and gives the players nearby Hero of the Village; a village that stops being one, a raid that outlasts 48000 ticks or one nobody is near ends it. Villagers read the raid state, so they take cover between waves and celebrate a win, and an active raid is saved with the level and carries on after a restart.
