Roll a dying mob's Java loot table with the mob and the blow that killed it, and pay out Java's death experience. A player's kill now unlocks the drops Java keeps for one, such as a blaze's rods, a sheep drops wool in its own color, and a slime drops slime balls only at the smallest size. A mob a player hurt drops experience orbs worth what Java's `getBaseExperienceReward` gives it, and one that died with no player involved drops none.
