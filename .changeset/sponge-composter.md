Sponges now soak up water and composters follow Java's rules. A sponge placed or updated next to water removes water within six steps, up to 65 blocks, and becomes a wet sponge; it did nothing. A composter took any item and always added a layer; it now accepts only compostable items and adds a layer with the item's chance, with the first item into an empty composter always landing.
