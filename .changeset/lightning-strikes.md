Make a lightning bolt strike. On its first tick it deals 5 lightning damage and 8 seconds of fire to every entity within 3 blocks, which runs the damage sensors that turn a pig into a zombified piglin, a villager into a witch or a creeper into a charged one, and it goes after three ticks. A transformation into the legacy `minecraft:pig_zombie` now spawns `minecraft:zombie_pigman`.
