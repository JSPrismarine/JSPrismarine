Send entities to clients from the change log. After each tick seals, `NetworkWorldReplicator` reads it into the entity replica and sends what it changed (`network/replica/EntityReplication.ts`): an entity that entered is shown to the clients in range as it entered, one that left is taken off every client, and only the entities whose fields or slots the tick wrote are visited for their equipment, metadata, health and moves. A first sight is built from the replica (`network/replica/firstSight.ts`) and encoded once for each codec and compression group. The world's entity tracking runs after the seal, and `World.shownEntities` and `WorldChangeSink.shownEntities` hand tracking the replica's entities as `ShownEntity` values.

Health now goes out with the maximum the store holds, so an entity whose maximum rose is no longer shown the one it spawned with ([#2995](https://github.com/JSPrismarine/JSPrismarine/issues/2995)).

Breaking changes: `StoreActors.sync` is gone, and `StoreActors.add` takes only the entity and no longer shows it. `WorldChangeSink` loses `entityAttributesChanged`, `entityMetadataChanged`, `entityEquipmentChanged`, `entityAdded` and `entityRemoved`, gains `flushEntities`, `flushDepartures` and `shownEntities`, and `reconcile` takes no entities. A world runs `flushEntities`, `flushOccurrences`, `flushDepartures` and `flushOwnerViews` after the seal, then its tracking pass, so an event about an entity that left, such as a killing blow's hurt flash, reaches the clients tracking it before its removal. `World` loses `shownActors`, `showActor`, `hideActor`, `sendActorMetadata` and `entityEquipmentChanged`. `PlayerSession` loses `sendAddActor`, `sendAddItemActor`, `sendAddPlayer`, `sendEquipmentFor` and `sendHeldItemFor`, and its tracking takes `ShownEntity` values. `ShownActor` loses `spawnTo` and `getTrackingRange`. A store actor's `metadata` and `attributes` are read from the store when asked, and `showPlayerState`, `showHealth` and `writeEquipment` are gone. `MobBody.teleportPending` is replaced by the logged `teleports` count. `actorMetadataInputs` and `sameActorMetadataInputs` are gone.

Entity writes, spawns and removals made between ticks, such as a console command's, are held and logged when the next tick opens. A store player's head yaw is logged as `look.headYaw`, and its entity enters the log with what clients are shown of it beside its saved form (`entity/playerShown.ts`).
