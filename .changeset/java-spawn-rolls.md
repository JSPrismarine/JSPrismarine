Roll Java's spawn tables in the vanilla entity files' spawn events. A spider takes a skeleton jockey one time in a hundred at any hour and in any biome, and never a stray, bogged, parched or wither skeleton; a cave spider never takes one. A strider takes a zombified piglin on one adult in 30 and a baby strider on one in ten of the rest. Rabbits draw brown, salt and pepper and black coats by Java's weights, white and white splotched in snowy biomes and gold in the desert. A fox is white in a biome tagged for snow foxes. A spawned villager has no profession. A tropical fish is one of Java's common variants nine times in ten, with Java's patterns and colors for the four Bedrock draws differently, and otherwise a random fish that may now be black. A sulfur cube spawned on its own is full size, and only its herd mates may spawn small.
