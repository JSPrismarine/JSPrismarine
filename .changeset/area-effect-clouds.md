Run area effect clouds. A cloud spawned with its numbers in its spawn data, such as the ender dragon's breath, now waits 20 ticks, then reaches whoever stands within its radius every fifth tick: instant damage hurts for 3 at the cloud's half strength and heals the undead instead, a lasting effect lands at its scaled duration, and nobody is reached again until 20 ticks have passed. The cloud is gone once its wait and its duration have run out.
