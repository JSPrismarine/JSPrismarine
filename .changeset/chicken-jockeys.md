Assemble chicken jockeys as Java's `Zombie.finalizeSpawn` does: a baby zombie, husk, drowned, zombie villager or zombified piglin that spawns climbs onto a free chicken within 5 blocks on a 0.05 roll, or else gets a chicken spawned under it on a second 0.05 roll.
