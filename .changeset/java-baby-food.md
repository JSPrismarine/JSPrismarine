Feed vanilla babies Java's foods by Java's amounts. A wolf pup eats fish and rabbit stew, a dolphin calf anything in `minecraft:fishes` and a baby bee open eyeblossom, each growing by a tenth of the time it has left. A llama or trader llama cria grows 10 seconds on wheat and 90 on a hay bale, and a camel calf 10 seconds per cactus, where they grew a tenth of their whole growth or more.
