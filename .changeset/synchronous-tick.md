Breaking: a tick runs in one synchronous call and awaits nothing. `World.update`, `Level.update`, `ServerTicker.advance` and `Server.advanceTick` return `void`, and `TickerServer.emitTick`, `admitInputs`, `tickFunctions` and `TickerWorld.update` no longer return promises the ticker waits for. Sends in a tick queue their packets and let the transport frame them later, and saves a tick starts are written off the tick.

Breaking: a plugin's listener for the `tick`, `entityPortalEnter` and `playerChangeDimension` events runs to its first wait inside the tick and is not waited for. A cancel made after an await no longer stops the portal contact or the change of dimension. `EventEmitter.post` takes an optional handler for the rejection of an asynchronous listener.

Breaking: `InputStep.defer` is gone, and `InputStep.output` hands work to the transport without the input step waiting for it. Input-lane handlers run commands through the new `CommandManager.runCommand`, which runs a command in one call and returns `{ ran, settled }`; `dispatchCommand` still settles once the command's work has. `HandlerContexts.run` takes connection-lane and read-lane handlers, and `runInput` applies an input-lane handler. `World.runInput`, `EntityStore.unitAsync` and `applyInput` are gone; use `runInputNow`, `unit` and `applyInputNow`.

Breaking: these now return their result directly instead of a promise: `RespawnRequests.admit`, `WorldMessages.admit`, `DimensionTravel.admit`, `touch` and `transfer`, `DragonFight.tick` and `setDragonKilled`, `ChunkAdmissions.admit` and `deactivate`, `NetworkDispatcher.admitInputs`, `CommandManager.tickFunctions`, `World.hitBlock`, `startBlockCracking`, `stopBlockCracking`, `dropFromBlock`, `removePlayer`, `sendTime` and `announceGameRule`, `PlayerSession.updatePlayer`, `update`, `completeDimensionChange`, `leave`, `stopMining` and `clearChunks`, `ClientConnection.closePlayerSession`, `BlockPhysics.update`, `explode` and `tickStatusEffects`. The `WorldChangeSink` tick phases return `void`, and a `ChunkCompletion.apply` returns its `boolean` directly. `PlayerSession.changeWorld`, `startCredits` and the other sends that still return a promise queue every packet before they return, and the promise only says when the transport framed them.

Breaking: `RespawnSearch.find`, `PlayerSession.getRespawnPoint`, `ChunkAdmissions.admitNow` and `admitParked`, `ChunkCompletionQueue.admitParked`, `TickStalledError`, `TICK_STALL_MS`, `IN_TICK_CHUNK_LOADS`, `currentTickScope` and the snapshot's `admittedInTick` are gone. `PlayerSession.attemptRespawnPoint` asks the respawn search without waiting, and `requestRespawnPoint` takes a synchronous answer. `trackTickWaits` and `currentTickWaitTracker` move to `world/TickWaits`.

A trip out of the End waits at the input step for the respawn search to have its chunks, and a trip's arrival chunks hold a chunk ticket until it lands, so an unload pass cannot take them first. Tick functions run as one input of the default world at the end of the input step, before the worlds update, as Java runs them before its levels. `PlayerPersistence` writes a player's records in the order they were started, and reading a record waits for its pending write. `Server` takes a `tickClock` for the loop's schedule and its phase timings.
