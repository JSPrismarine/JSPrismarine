Snow golems, ravagers and breezes run their Java rules. A snow golem leaves snow in the air around its feet wherever snow can lie, with `mobGriefing` on. A ravager that walks into leaves breaks them, and only leaves, and jumps when there were none to break. An arrow or other projectile that reaches a breeze turns back at half its speed and does it no harm.
