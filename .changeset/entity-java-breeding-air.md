Breed and drown mobs by Java's rules. Animals now breed with the items in Java's food tags, so wolves take fish and rabbit stew and bees take open eyeblossom, and a horse fed an enchanted golden apple falls in love instead of ignoring the item's old Bedrock name. Mobs hold Java's 15 seconds of air underwater, which a bee, a happy ghast and a tadpole lacked. A magma cube no longer catches fire in daylight.
