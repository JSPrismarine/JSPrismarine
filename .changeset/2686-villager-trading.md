Villagers trade with players: the trade screen opens on a villager, shows its offers with their live prices, and a trade spends the payment, hands over the result and moves the villager's stock, demand, experience and level. A villager that works at its job site restocks, up to twice a day. Villagers also work with the world: farmers bake bread, compost seeds, harvest, sow and bone-meal crops, villagers share food, thank a hero of the village and have children, and a villager takes its profession and job site from its trade table.
