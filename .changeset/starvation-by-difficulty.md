Starve a player by the world's difficulty, as Java's `FoodData.tick` does: an empty food bar stops hurting at 10 health on Easy and at 1 on Normal, and kills only on Hard. On Peaceful the food bar no longer drains, and with natural regeneration on a player gains a point of food every half second and heals half a heart every second.
