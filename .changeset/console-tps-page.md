The console has a TPS page. Click the TPS graph in the details pane or the TPS value in the status bar, or press T while the log has focus, to see up to 15 minutes of readings in a chart that covers the log. The page shows the latest, average, lowest, and highest TPS for a span of 1, 5, or 15 minutes, switched with the number keys, the arrow keys, or a click, and closes with T, Q, Esc, or its close control. The status bar now colors TPS green from 19, yellow from 15, and red below, and colors the tick time yellow from 40 ms and red from 50 ms. The details pane shows the lowest recent TPS and marks the graph as clickable.
