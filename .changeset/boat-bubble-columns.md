Move boats over bubble columns as Java does. A boat floating over the top of a bubble column now counts 60 ticks, then a rising column throws it up and a sinking one drags it under and drops its riders. A column under water pushes or pulls a boat inside it. Boats float on bubble columns as on water, and a boat over a sinking column keeps its seats, where the vanilla pack's timer took them away after three seconds.
