Every live player is a store entity. A `Player` holds a `StorePlayer` from its login on, whose `minecraft:player` entity enters its world's entity store when the client reports it has spawned and leaves it on a world change or a logout. Blows on a player run the store's damage pipeline, so a plugin's `entityDamage` handler, the grace period and armor apply to a player as to a mob, and a creative or spectator player is spared by the pipeline's `game_mode` guard. Breath, hunger, falls, meals, the void and mining cracks run in the store's player systems. Armor and off-hand changes now reach other players at once. A pack stack that declares no `minecraft:player` gets a built-in one, so a server started without the Bedrock pack still gives its players an entity.

Breaking: `Player.getBody()` returns a `ShownPlayer`, the player's `StorePlayerActor` while it is in a store and a detached view before it spawns. `Player.getActor()` returns the `StorePlayerActor`, or `undefined` before the spawn and between two worlds; pass it where entity code takes a player, and compare `event.getEntity()` from `entityDamage` or `entityDeath` with it. `World.getEntity()` and `World.getEntities()` no longer hold players; `World.getActor()` and `World.getActors()` answer a player's actor. `LegacyPlayerBody`, `Human`, the `Entities` namespace export with its `Human` and `Player` members, `Entity.restoreLoggedField` and `Entity.restoreLoggedSlot` are removed. `Player`'s `pitch`, `yaw` and `headYaw` have no setters, `xuid` is read-only, and `Player.isSprinting()` replaces reading `player.metadata.sprinting`.
