Ship built-in definitions for `minecraft:item` and `minecraft:eye_of_ender_signal` in the entity overlay, with the `jsprismarine:item` and `jsprismarine:eye_of_ender_signal` components that carry their tuning. Mojang's pack has no file for either, and a pack later in the stack that ships one replaces it.
