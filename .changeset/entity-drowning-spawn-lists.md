Drown mobs once a second and spawn Java's mobs in every biome. A mob out of air takes its drowning blow every second, as in both editions, rather than every half second. The entity overlay now lets hostile mobs spawn in rivers, frozen rivers and snowy plains, skeletons in mangrove swamps, rabbits in old growth taigas and bats in old growth birch forests, as Java's biome spawn lists do.
