Apply Java's rules to three vanilla entity files as they load: a dried-out axolotl is hurt 2 a second, as `Axolotl.handleAirSupply` does, instead of 1 every half second; a TNT minecart explodes with Java's power 4 instead of 3; and a creaking is no longer immune to freezing, which Java's creaking is not.
