Define and run the painting, the leash knot and the ominous item spawner. Paintings and leash knots now spawn: each holds still and, on Java's 100-tick check, breaks when its wall or fence is gone, a painting dropping its item. An ominous item spawner lets its item go between 60 and 120 ticks after it appears, firing an arrow or another projectile item straight down and dropping anything else, and then leaves.
