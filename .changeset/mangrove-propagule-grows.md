Grow a planted mangrove propagule into a mangrove tree on a random tick in the light, and age a hanging one every random tick whatever the light, as Java's `MangrovePropaguleBlock` does. A propagule used to count up its stage and never grow. A sapling or propagule whose trunk has anything but air in its way does not grow, so a block above it is never overwritten.
