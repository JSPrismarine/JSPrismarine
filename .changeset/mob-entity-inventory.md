Every mob carries an `EntityInventory` in the mob layout, `MobBody.inventory`: one-slot `mainhand`, `offhand` and `body` containers, four `armor` slots and an `inventory` container sized from its `minecraft:inventory`. Each slot write is logged as an `inventory-slot` change entry and undone through `MobBody.restoreSlot`, as a player's are, and a change of the container's size is the logged body field `inventorySize`. `MobBody.equipment`, `setEquipment`, `carried`, `inventory` (the identifier list), `setInventory`, `containerSlots`, `setContainerSlot` and `setContainerSlots` are gone, and so are `DataEntity.carried` and `DataEntity.equipment`: read and write `body.inventory.equipment(slot)`, `body.inventory.setEquipment(slot, item)` and `body.inventory.container('inventory')`, and set a goal's held item with `EntityRuntime.setHeldItem`. `containerCapacity` is read-only and `setContainerCapacity` resizes the container. `equipmentInventorySlots` now points the equipment slot at its container slot instead of keeping a copy of the stack, so a horse's armor is one stack. `onEquipmentChanged` receives the slot and whether it holds something. `EntityView` no longer carries `equipment`; `EntityReplica.equipmentAddresses` says which slot each equipment slot reads. In `entity/equipment`, `NO_EQUIPMENT`, `withoutEquipmentSlot`, `sameEquipment` and `viewableEquipmentOf` are gone. In `entity/data/persistence`, `equipmentOf` and `containerSlotsOf` are no longer exported, `savedDropOf` and `equipmentDropsOf` are, `DataEntityRecord.inventory` is optional, and `applyDataEntityRecord` takes the content. Saved records keep the `equipment`, `containerSlots` and `equipmentInventorySlots` keys in the same shapes and no longer write `carried` or `inventory`; a record that has them loads them into the main hand and the container. A mob takes off the ground only what its hand and container can keep, and a stack given to it that does not fit drops at its feet. `ItemContainer.room` says how much of a stack fits, and `inventory/persistence` exports `dropOfItem`.
