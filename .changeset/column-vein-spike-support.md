Break the last blocks that floated without support, as Java does. A sculk vein pops once no face it covers is held, a sulfur spike pops without the block it grows from, as pointed dripstone does, and a bell on a wall pops without that wall. A bubble column now follows the block below it: it drags down over magma, pushes up over soul sand, and turns back into water over anything else.
