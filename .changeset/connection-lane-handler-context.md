The connection-lane handlers of a player session take a `SessionContext` in place of the `Server` and the session: `ActorPickRequestHandler`, `BlockPickRequestHandler`, `ClientCacheBlobStatusHandler`, `ClientCacheStatusHandler`, `ClientCameraAimAssistHandler`, `ClientMovementPredictionSyncHandler`, `CommandBlockUpdateHandler`, `EmoteListHandler`, `MapCreateLockedCopyHandler`, `MapInfoRequestHandler`, `ModalFormResponseHandler`, `MultiplayerSettingsHandler`, `NpcRequestHandler`, `PacketViolationWarningHandler`, `PartyChangedHandler`, `PhotoTransferHandler`, `PlayerLocationHandler`, `PositionTrackingDBClientRequestHandler`, `PurchaseReceiptHandler`, `ResourcePacksReadyForValidationHandler`, `ServerSettingsRequestHandler`, `ServerboundDiagnosticsHandler`, `ServerboundPackSettingChangeHandler`, `SetActorLinkHandler`, `SetDifficultyHandler`, `SetPlayerFurnaceOptionsHandler`, `SetPlayerInventoryOptionsHandler`, `SimpleEventHandler`, `SubClientLoginHandler`, `TickingAreasLoadStatusHandler` and `UpdateClientOptionsHandler`. A plugin that calls one of their `handle` methods directly passes a context. What each handler does is unchanged.
