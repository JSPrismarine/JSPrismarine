Deal Java's 5 lightning damage whatever a mob's damage sensor says of lightning, so a creeper takes it as it becomes charged and an axolotl no longer dies of a pack multiplier of 2000. A pig, a villager, a mooshroom and a turtle, whose Java `thunderHit` replaces the damage with a conversion, a variant change or a death, keep what their sensor does.
