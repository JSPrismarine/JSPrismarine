Use Command for the console's own shortcuts on macOS: `⌘K` opens the command palette, `⌘B` toggles the details pane, `⌘F` cycles the level filter and `⇧⌘R` restarts the server, and the help overlay, the palette and hints spell keys with the macOS symbols. The Control forms keep working, since most macOS terminals keep Command shortcuts for themselves. Copy and paste list `⌘C` and `⌘V` on macOS and `Ctrl+Shift+C` and `Ctrl+Shift+V` elsewhere.
