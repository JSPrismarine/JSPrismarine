A Java Edition player sees the players and mobs around them, which come into and leave their view and tab list and move as the server moves them. A session's presenter now names its edition and shows and hides entities, and the replicator hands a non-Bedrock viewer its moves as plain data for its presenter to encode. `@jsprismarine/protocol` gains Java's entity and player info packets and 26.3's entity type and item registries, generated by `dump:java-protocol`.
