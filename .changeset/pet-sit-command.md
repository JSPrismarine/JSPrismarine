Let an owner sit a tamed wolf, cat or parrot down and stand it back up. When no component takes the used item, the owner's use flips a pet with `minecraft:sittable` between sitting and standing and drops its attack target, as Java's `TamableAnimal.setOrderedToSit` does; a parrot only sits from the ground.
