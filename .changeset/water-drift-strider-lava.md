Keep a mob's momentum in water: once a swim stops it drifts on, keeping Java's share of its speed each tick (0.8, 0.9 for the fish, dolphin, guardians, turtle, axolotl, frog, tadpole, drowned and nautiluses, 0.96 for the skeleton horse and 0.98 for the polar bear), where it stopped dead. A strider now stands on a lava pool's surface and floats up out of deep lava, as Java's `Strider.floatStrider` does, instead of sinking.
