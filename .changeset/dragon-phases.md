Fly the ender dragon. Its phase goals now run Java's phase manager: the dragon laps the node rings around the fountain, strafes a player with a dragon fireball, charges where a player stood, lands on the exit portal, scans for a player, roars and breathes a cloud of dragon breath that hurts for 3, takes off again after four breaths or once it has lost a quarter of its health while perched, and flies by Java's flight model with no gravity. The phase is saved with the dragon. `GoalWorld` gains `moveSelf`, `surfaceY`, `seaLevel`, `fightOrigin`, `endCrystalsAlive`, `discard` and `hasLineOfSight`.
