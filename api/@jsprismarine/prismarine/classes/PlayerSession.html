changeWorld() ​

changeWorld(arrival, options?): Promise<void>

Defined in: prismarine/src/network/PlayerSession.ts:1984

Moves the player to another world and walks the client through the dimension change.

Server side first: the record is saved to the world being left, stamped with that world's dimension, the player leaves its roster, the old world's entities come off the client, the chunk bookkeeping is cleared, the position moves, and the player joins the new roster. Then the wire sequence Dragonfly, Geyser and Nukkit agree on: one ChangeDimension with the target's Bedrock id, StopSound for everything playing, NetworkChunkPublisherUpdate at the arrival, the server-sent PlayerAction ChangeDimensionAck, the arrival chunks, and PlayStatus PlayerSpawn. The change stays pending, and the client's PlayerAuthInput is dropped, until the client closes the screen with the id this handed out: see PlayerSession.completeDimensionChange.

A client sent ChangeDimension to the dimension it is already in never closes the screen, so a move between two worlds of the same Bedrock id detours through another dimension first.

A second arrival while a change is pending is held until the client closes the first screen, then carried out; only the latest is kept. A caller whose arrival goes stale while it waits, such as a portal, instead passes whenPending: 'drop': the player is still ticked on the loading screen, and one who arrived inside the exit portal would otherwise be sent back once the 10 tick cooldown ran out, before the client ever closed the first screen. Custom dimension ids are sent after the level announces them in DimensionData.

Parameters ​

arrival ​

Arrival

The world, feet position and rotation the player lands at.

options? ​

What to do with this arrival when a change is already pending.

"queue" | "drop"

clearChunks() ​

clearChunks(): Promise<void>

Defined in: prismarine/src/network/PlayerSession.ts:1107

Clear the currently loaded and loading chunks.

Returns ​

Promise<void>

Remarks ​

Usually used for changing dimension, world, etc.

Everything in flight goes with them. The two sets are only the bookkeeping; the queue of coordinates still to send and the chunks already produced but not yet dispatched are the actual work, and they belong to the world being left. Left in place, a dimension change sent the old world's terrain into the new one, and resolved the leftover coordinates against a world where they mean somewhere else entirely.

openBlockContainer() ​

openBlockContainer(at, identifier, contents?): Promise<OpenBlockContainer | undefined>

Defined in: prismarine/src/network/PlayerSession.ts:1253

Opens the screen a block draws, with whatever the block is holding.

The one call the interaction path needs. Until this existed no block opened a container anywhere in this server: a chest, a furnace and a crafting table all did nothing when they were clicked, because openMainInventory was the only ContainerOpen ever sent.

The contents follow the open for the same reason they do there: a window whose contents never arrive is one the client has no reason to keep. Each packet uses the container name that screen assigns to its slots, so a furnace's fuel list arrives under its fuel name. A workstation stores nothing of its own, so no contents follow its open. A crafting table is followed by the player's own inventory under window 0 instead, which is what a Bedrock Dedicated Server 1.26.52 sent after a click on one.
