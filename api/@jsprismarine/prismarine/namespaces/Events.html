EntityDamageEvent ​

Defined in: prismarine/src/events/entity/EntityDamageEvent.ts:14

Fired just before an entity is hurt, and before anything has been taken off its health.

Cancellable, and the amount is writable, so a plugin can make a region safe or a mob tougher without having to intercept every source of damage separately. The amount here is the raw blow: armor, enchantments and resistance have not been applied yet, which is what makes it the useful number to change - doubling it doubles the hit as a player would understand it, rather than undoing whatever their armor was about to do.

EntityDeathEvent ​

Defined in: prismarine/src/events/entity/EntityDeathEvent.ts:13

Fired once, when an entity's health has reached zero.

Not cancellable: by the time this runs the health is already gone and the client has been told, so a listener that "canceled" it would leave a corpse walking around with no hearts. What it is for is reacting - loot, statistics, an announcement - and for that, knowing who did it is the whole point, which is why the source is carried rather than only the cause.

EntityPortalEnterEvent ​

Defined in: prismarine/src/events/entity/EntityPortalEnterEvent.ts:12

Fired when an entity's body first overlaps a portal block, before any contact timer starts.

Cancelable. A canceled event opens no contact, so the entity stays where it is; the world asks again on the next tick it still overlaps the portal. Players raise this too, and then PlayerChangeDimensionEvent once their timer runs out and a destination is known.

PlayerChangeDimensionEvent ​

Defined in: prismarine/src/events/player/PlayerChangeDimensionEvent.ts:14

Fired just before a player leaves one world for another through a portal, with the destination resolved.

Cancelable. A canceled event leaves the player in the world they stand in, with no cooldown set and nothing sent to the client. The arrival is the resolved landing place, so a listener can read where the trip would end before deciding.
